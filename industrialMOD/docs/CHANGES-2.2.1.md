# Industrial Technology 2.2.1

The Green Redstone Lamp now has plain, solid green faces. The previous metal casing and panel details are removed. Without power it is green; with a redstone signal it becomes bright green and emits light level 15. Vanilla lighting is unchanged; the glowing surface is green.

Its original registry identity, recipe, existing placed blocks, and support for levers on all six faces remain. Redstone behavior and all other machinery are unchanged from 2.2.0. Space content remains removed. No user world or installed game mod was edited.
